# Demo Scenario
CariKOL — Presentation Script
Last updated: 2026-07-07

**Purpose:** A concrete 5-minute demo that makes every abstract concept (Decision, Knowledge, Outcome Review, Brand Memory) visible and felt — not just described.

Judges and reviewers will not read 12 documents. They will watch 5 minutes. This scenario is designed so that by the end of those 5 minutes, the viewer has experienced the core loop themselves.

---

## The Story

**The problem in one sentence:**
A campaign manager opens a new brief and spends 45 minutes searching old spreadsheets and WhatsApp before they can propose a single creator.

**The product in one sentence:**
CariKOL means that same campaign manager opens the platform and has the answers in 30 seconds — because every past campaign already built that knowledge.

**The demo shows this transformation.**

---

## Pre-Demo Setup

Before the demo, the database must contain:
- 1 existing brand: **Wardah**
- 5+ past campaigns for Wardah with completed Decision and OutcomeReview records
- 300 creator records with varied history, ratings, and rejection records
- At least 3 creators with specific Wardah history (1 previously confirmed + rated, 1 previously rejected by client, 1 with no-response history)
- 1 logged-in user: "Dina — Campaign Manager"

The demo should use real-looking Indonesian data. Creator names, rate ranges in IDR, realistic niches.

---

## Scene 1 — The Brief (60 seconds)

**What the viewer sees:**
Dina opens CariKOL. The Dashboard shows two active campaigns and one campaign pending Outcome Review. She clicks "New Campaign."

A structured brief form opens. She fills in:
- Brand: **Wardah**
- Platform: TikTok
- Objective: Awareness for new skincare line, usia 18–25
- Creator criteria: Beauty / Skincare, Micro tier (100K–500K followers)
- Budget: IDR 800K – 2.5M per creator
- Deliverables: 1 TikTok video per creator, 15–30 detik
- Content deadline: 20 July 2026

She clicks "Create Campaign." The campaign is created in Draft status.

**What this demonstrates:**
- Standardized brief — no more inconsistent Google Docs
- One place, structured, searchable

---

## Scene 2 — Creator Workspace (90 seconds)

**What the viewer sees:**
KOL Specialist Reza opens the campaign and clicks "Add Creators." The Creator Workspace opens.

He filters:
- Platform: TikTok
- Niche: Beauty / Skincare
- Tier: Micro
- Rate: under IDR 2.5M

The list loads. **Each creator card shows:**
- Follower count, engagement rate
- Average rating from past campaigns
- "Would work again" indicator

Then — this is the key moment — **Wardah context appears automatically** on three cards:

```
🟢 Aurel S.     | 280K followers | ⭐ 4.8 avg | ✅ Previously confirmed for Wardah — rated 5/5
🔴 Reni A.      | 195K followers | ⭐ 4.1 avg | ⚠️ Previously rejected by Wardah — "audience not matching"
🟡 Bowo K.      | 310K followers | ⭐ 3.9 avg | ❗ No response in 2 of last 3 campaigns
```

Reza clicks **Aurel S.** to see her full profile. The full decision history appears:
- Campaign 1 (Wardah, March 2026) — Confirmed, rate IDR 1.8M, rated 5/5, "would work again"
- Campaign 2 (Brand X, January 2026) — Confirmed, rate IDR 1.5M, rated 4/5

He adds Aurel to the campaign. He skips Reni (already rejected by Wardah before). He adds 4 others.

He marks the list as "Ready for Internal Review."

**What this demonstrates:**
- Knowledge surfacing at the moment of decision — no searching required
- Brand context appears automatically — not manually looked up
- 2 minutes to build a creator list that previously took 45 minutes

---

## Scene 3 — Internal Review and Decision Capture (60 seconds)

**What the viewer sees:**
Dina switches to Internal Review mode. She sees the 5 proposed creators.

She reviews one creator — **Indah B., 220K followers, beauty niche.** No Wardah history. She decides to remove Indah — rate is outside budget.

She clicks "Reject." A small decision form appears:
```
Reason:          [ Rate exceeds budget ▾ ]
Confidence:      ● Explicit  ○ Inferred
Notes:           (optional)
```

She selects "Rate exceeds budget," leaves confidence as Explicit, and clicks Confirm.

Indah is removed from the active list. The decision is recorded.

Dina approves the remaining 4 creators and clicks "Send to Client."

**What this demonstrates:**
- Rejection requires a structured reason — the knowledge is captured automatically
- It takes 3 clicks, not a paragraph
- The decision is now permanently searchable

---

## Scene 4 — Client Feedback (45 seconds)

**What the viewer sees:**
Dina receives WhatsApp feedback from Wardah: "Please replace Tika W., she already worked with our competitor recently."

She opens CariKOL and logs the client's feedback.

She finds Tika W. in the list and clicks "Client Rejected."
```
Reason:          [ Previously worked with competitor ▾ ]
Confidence:      ● Explicit  ○ Inferred
Notes:           Client mentioned this in WhatsApp
```

She selects Explicit. Confirms. Tika is removed.

**What this demonstrates:**
- Client feedback is logged with confidence level — the system knows this was stated explicitly, not assumed
- Next time Tika appears for any Wardah campaign, this history will surface automatically

---

## Scene 5 — Campaign Close and Knowledge Update (60 seconds)

**What the viewer sees:**
(Fast-forward — campaign ran successfully. Dina initiates close.)

She clicks "Close Campaign." The Outcome Review screen opens for each confirmed creator.

She completes Aurel S.'s review in 20 seconds:
```
Content Quality:   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Communication:     ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Professionalism:   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Would work again:  ✅ Yes
Notes:             Delivered early, excellent caption
```

She completes the remaining 3 creators. She clicks "Complete Close."

The system confirms:
> "Campaign closed. Creator records updated. Brand Memory updated for Wardah."

She navigates to Aurel S.'s creator profile. Her rating has just increased. Her Wardah history now shows two confirmed campaigns.

**The final shot:**
Dina opens a new campaign for Wardah. Aurel S. appears at the top of the Creator Workspace search — with a green badge, 5/5 rating, and "Confirmed for Wardah × 2."

**What this demonstrates:**
- Campaign close IS the knowledge event
- The next campaign for Wardah is already smarter than the last
- The loop is complete: Campaign → Decision → Knowledge → Better Decision

> "Every time Buzzeryok started a new campaign, the team spent 30–60 minutes searching spreadsheets and WhatsApp to find information they already had. This is what that looks like with CariKOL."

> "The system knows Aurel worked well with Wardah before. It knows Reni was rejected by the client. It knows Bowo doesn't always respond. That knowledge didn't require anyone to write a report — it was captured automatically from past campaigns."

**After Scene 3 (10 seconds):**
> "When Dina rejects a creator, she selects a reason. One click. That reason is now permanently searchable — not in a spreadsheet, not in a WhatsApp chat. In the system."

**After Scene 5 (20 seconds):**
> "When the campaign closes, the knowledge updates. Aurel's record gets better. Wardah's Brand Memory gets richer. The next campaign for Wardah will start with more context than this one — automatically. That's the loop: every campaign makes the next one smarter."

## What NOT To Show in the Demo

```
❌ Settings page
❌ Admin functions
❌ Empty states
❌ Error handling
❌ Loading states or slow queries
❌ Any feature not in the WE ARE BUILDING list
❌ Anything that requires explanation longer than one sentence
```

The demo should feel like watching someone work, not watching a product tour.
