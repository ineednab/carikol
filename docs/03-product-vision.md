# Product Vision
CariKOL — Pre-PRD Synthesis
Last updated: 2026-07-07

---

## The One Sentence

> Every campaign should leave the organization smarter than before it started.

---

## Vision Statement

CariKOL transforms completed campaigns into compounding organizational intelligence.

Every decision made during a campaign — who was approved, who was rejected, why, how the creator performed, how the client behaved — should make the next campaign faster, smarter, and less dependent on individual memory.

---

## What the Product Is

CariKOL is a **campaign workspace**.

That is what users see, what users experience, and what users adopt. It is the place where campaigns are run. It is familiar, operational, and fast.

What makes it different from every other campaign tool is invisible to users:

- Every workflow action captures a structured decision
- Every decision accumulates into organizational knowledge
- That knowledge is surfaced contextually at the moment the next similar decision is being made
- Over time, the organization becomes measurably smarter

The knowledge system is the engine. The campaign workspace is the product.

---

## The Core Loop

```
Campaign opens
      ↓
Decisions are made (approve, reject, replace, negotiate, evaluate)
      ↓
Decisions are captured — automatically, as a byproduct of completing workflow steps
      ↓
Campaign closes with mandatory Outcome Review
      ↓
Knowledge is updated: Creator Reputation + Brand Memory + Campaign Pattern
      ↓
Campaign is Closed — knowledge permanently embedded in the system
      ↓
Next campaign opens with richer context than the last
      ↓
Better decisions are made
      ↓
Knowledge compounds
```

## Long-Term Vision

CariKOL becomes the operational operating system for influencer marketing agencies.

The accumulated intelligence of hundreds of campaigns — creator behavioral patterns, brand preferences, rate histories, campaign strategies — becomes the most defensible competitive advantage a business can have.

Competitors can scrape TikTok data. They cannot replicate four years of operational decisions embedded in an organization's knowledge base.

---

## MVP Vision

CariKOL MVP is the smallest version of this loop that delivers immediate, demonstrable value to Buzzeryok's internal team.

A campaign workspace for KOL campaigns where every decision made during pre-execution is captured as structured, searchable organizational knowledge — and where that knowledge surfaces automatically the next time a similar decision needs to be made.

**The MVP is successful when:**
- A KOL Specialist finds relevant creator history in seconds instead of an hour
- Every rejection has a documented reason, not a WhatsApp message
- Every closed campaign has enriched the creator records of everyone who participated
- Three months after launch, a new campaign for Brand X surfaces which creators worked with Brand X before, which were rejected and why, and which performed best

## The Knowledge Maturity Model

CariKOL is built in knowledge maturity phases, not feature phases.

AI does not appear as a feature. It appears as the consequence of accumulated knowledge.

```
Level 1 — Data
          Creators, campaigns, and brands exist as structured records.
          The foundation is built.

Level 2 — Workflow
          Campaigns move through a structured lifecycle.
          Every step produces consistent, structured output.

Level 3 — Decision                              ← MVP delivers Levels 1, 2, 3
          Every workflow action captures a decision with reason and confidence.
          The system knows not just what happened, but why it happened.

Level 4 — Knowledge                             ← Emerges from consistent MVP use
          Decisions accumulate into Brand Memory and Creator Reputation.
          Questions that took an hour now take seconds.

Level 5 — Intelligence                          ← Designed for, not yet built
          The knowledge base is rich enough to surface recommendations,
          detect patterns, and eventually automate decisions.
          AI emerges naturally from the data. It is not added.
```

## Positioning (Internal)

CariKOL is a decision intelligence system operating as a campaign workspace.

The workflow is the mechanism.
The decision is the atomic unit of value.
The knowledge base is the long-term moat.

*This framing is for the product team. To users, CariKOL is the place where campaigns run. The intelligence layer should feel like "the system just knows things" — not like a separate product.*
