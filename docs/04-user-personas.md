# User Personas
CariKOL — Pre-PRD Synthesis
Last updated: 2026-07-07

---

## Persona 1 — Campaign Manager

**Role:** Primary decision-maker and coordinator for every campaign
**Usage frequency:** Daily
**CariKOL classification:** Primary power user

### Responsibilities
- Receives client briefs and translates them into operational instructions
- Reviews KOL Specialist's creator proposals
- Makes final internal decisions on creator approval and rejection
- Manages client approval process
- Tracks campaign progress across all stakeholders
- Closes campaigns and evaluates outcomes

### Current pain points
- Campaign briefs are inconsistently structured — different campaigns documented differently
- Creator review happens in WhatsApp with no documented reasoning
- Cannot quickly answer "which creators have we worked with for Brand X?"
- Every revision round rebuilds the spreadsheet manually
- Campaign knowledge disappears when the campaign closes
- Spends significant time following up manually across channels

### What they need from CariKOL
- One place that holds the complete state of every active campaign
- Creator workspace where decisions can be made with historical context visible
- Structured brief template so every campaign starts the same way
- Decision capture that requires minimum additional effort
- Outcome Review that closes the knowledge loop without adding administrative burden

### What will make them abandon CariKOL
- Any workflow that adds more steps than their current WhatsApp process
- A system that requires documentation before it provides value
- Slow or complicated UI during time-pressured campaign moments

---

## Persona 2 — KOL Specialist

**Role:** Sources, proposes, and manages creator relationships
**Usage frequency:** Daily
**CariKOL classification:** Primary daily user

### Responsibilities
- Searches and identifies creators matching campaign criteria
- Builds initial creator lists for Campaign Manager review
- Contacts creators individually to confirm availability and rates
- Manages creator communication during negotiation
- Updates creator information after each campaign

### Current pain points
- Every campaign requires rebuilding creator lists from scratch
- Historical creator information is scattered across old spreadsheets and WhatsApp
- Cannot easily find which creators worked well with specific brands
- No visibility into why creators were rejected on past campaigns
- Rate history is not centralized — must chase old files or remember verbally

### What they need from CariKOL
- Searchable creator workspace with filters (platform, niche, rate, past campaigns, past brands)
- Creator profiles that accumulate decision history automatically
- Ability to build and propose a creator list without touching a spreadsheet
- Visibility into what the Campaign Manager has previously approved or rejected for similar campaigns

### What will make them abandon CariKOL
- If searching the creator workspace is slower than opening a spreadsheet
- If the creator data at launch is too sparse to be useful
- If they must manually re-enter data they already have in spreadsheets

## Persona 3 — Finance (Out of MVP Scope)

**Role:** Invoicing and payment tracking
**Usage frequency:** Per campaign close
**CariKOL classification:** Future phase user

### Notes
Finance needs are real but not in MVP scope. The campaign close event and Outcome Review can eventually trigger financial workflows. For now, Finance continues using existing tools.

---

## Persona 4 — Client (Out of MVP Scope)

**Role:** Campaign requester and creator approver
**Usage frequency:** Per campaign
**CariKOL classification:** Never in MVP

Clients continue to receive creator lists via WhatsApp, Google Sheets, or PDF. Client feedback continues to be logged manually by the Campaign Manager inside CariKOL. A client portal is a future phase decision that would triple the scope of the product and introduce external adoption challenges.

---

## Persona 5 — Creator / Contributor (Out of MVP Scope)

**Role:** Campaign executor
**Usage frequency:** Per campaign
**CariKOL classification:** Never in MVP

Creators continue to receive briefs and submit content via WhatsApp and Google Drive. Their behavioral data (reliability, communication quality, rate patterns) is captured inside CariKOL by the Campaign Manager and KOL Specialist based on direct interaction. Creators never log in during MVP.

---

## Adoption Risk Summary

The two users who must adopt CariKOL for the product to succeed are the Campaign Manager and the KOL Specialist.

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Tool adds more work than WhatsApp | Every workflow step must be faster than the manual equivalent |
| Creator database starts empty | Migrate ~300 quality records before launch |
| Knowledge capture feels like paperwork | Capture knowledge only through required workflow actions, not separate documentation steps |
| Team reverts to spreadsheets | The creator search must be demonstrably faster than opening a spreadsheet from Day 1 |
